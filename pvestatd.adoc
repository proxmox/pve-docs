pvestatd - Proxmox VE Status Daemon
===================================
endif::manvolnum[]

This daemon queries the status of VMs, storages and containers at
regular intervals. The result is sent to all nodes in the cluster.

The daemon also reports the multipath status of the node to the cluster. On
nodes with `multipath-tools` installed, it first applies the cluster-wide
multipath configuration: it writes the `/etc/multipath/conf.d/pve-*.conf`
files, adds the WWIDs of managed LUNs to `/etc/multipath/wwids`, and reloads
`multipathd` after a change.
